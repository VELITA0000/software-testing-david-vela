   - Valid card numbers: Length between 13 and 16 digits, containing only numeric digits.

    Case | Description                                  | Input                   | Expected Output
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      4  | Invalid: Too long (More than 16)             | 12345678901234567       | False
      5  | Invalid: Contains non-numeric characters     | 1234ABCD90123           | False

BOUNDARY VALUE ANALYSIS

1. Function that calculates the eligibility of a person for a loan based on their income and credit score.
   The eligibility rules are as follows:
   - If the income is less than $30,000, the person is not eligible for a loan.
   - If the income is between $30,000 and $60,000 (inclusive) and the credit score is above 700, the person is eligible for a standard loan.
   - If the income is between $30,000 and $60,000 (inclusive) and the credit score is below or equal to 700, the person is eligible for a secured loan.
   - If the income is greater than $60,000 and the credit score is above 750, the person is eligible for a premium loan.
   - If the income is greater than $60,000 and the credit score is between 700 and 750 (inclusive), the person is eligible for a standard loan.

    Case | Description (Income, Credit Score)         | (Income - Credit Score)   | Expected Output
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      1  | Income just below 30k                      | $29,999 - 750             | Not Eligible
      2  | Income at lower bound, CS just below 700   | $30,000 - 699             | Secured Loan
      3  | Income at lower bound, CS at boundary      | $30,000 - 700             | Secured Loan
      4  | Income at lower bound, CS just above 700	  | $30,000 - 701             |  Standard Loan
      5  | Income at upper bound, CS just below 700   | $60,000 - 699             | Secured Loan
      6  | Income at upper bound, CS at boundary      | $60,000 - 700             | Secured Loan
      7  | Income at upper bound, CS just above 700   | $60,000 - 701             | Standard Loan
      8  | Income just above 60k, CS just below 750   | $60,001 - 749             | Standard Loan
      9  | Income just above 60k, CS at boundary      | $60,001 - 750             | Standard Loan
      10 | Income just above 60k, CS just above 750   | $60,001 - 751             | Premium Loan
